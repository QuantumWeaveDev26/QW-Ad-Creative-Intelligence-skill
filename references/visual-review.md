# Ad image review and revision
Inspect the actual output. Mark each material criterion ready, needs revision, or unverified. Do not invent a numeric conversion score.

## Block delivery as a finished ad when
- The product, packaging, logo, person or required invariant changed incorrectly.
- Headline/price/qualification/CTA is misspelled, clipped, unreadable, mistranslated or differs from approved text.
- It implies an unsupported result, fake customer endorsement, statistic, guarantee or real screenshot.
- The image contradicts the offer or destination, or hides a material condition.
- The requested generation/edit was never actually executed.
Repair these faults or label the deliverable incomplete. A demo is allowed when requested and labelled; it is not genuine product proof.

## Improve the creative argument
Can a viewer identify the relevant product/problem and main message quickly? Does the visual demonstrate the angle rather than just decorate it? Is there a clear focal point and scan order at a phone-sized preview? Is the qualification useful without clutter? Does the CTA lead to the promised next step? Is the style appropriate for this business and format rather than a generic template? Does every prominent element earn its space?

## Check execution
Inspect text in the actual requested language, product labels, hands/geometry where relevant, edge quality, light/shadows/perspective, brand/character continuity, copy contrast, crop margins and requested transparency. A fictional stylized image need not pretend photorealism. Check actual dimensions where available; leave platform acceptance unverified until current specs and upload checks are performed.

## Revise with intent
State the concrete fault and change only what repairs it: e.g. shorten a headline without changing the claim; move text away from a crop boundary; restore the supplied label; remove an unverified badge; replace an irrelevant visual metaphor. Preserve product, message and successful composition. Reinspect the edited result.

## Deliver with accurate status
Return the final rendered artifact and matching ad fields. Separate concept approval, visual QA, factual/brand approval, placement readiness and live performance. Never claim all five from a generation response. If the user asked for no spending/credits and rendering cannot be confirmed within that boundary, supply the concept and state rendering is pending rather than spending or pretending to render.



## Compare to the original reference
Confirm the exact SKU/edition, not merely the general product category. Check identity details at a readable scale and state when the source resolution is too low to approve them. Visually consistent is not pixel-identical. Model-added props are acceptable only when they support the concept and do not imply extra products, included accessories, endorsements or unsupported performance. Preserve product identity over decorative styling. Record generator-canvas dimensions and leave actual platform upload acceptance pending until checked.

For detailed products, record a few identity-critical landmarks before generation: model marking, regional keyboard/label variant, screen content, control rows, closure or artwork, and overall proportions. After generation record expected detail, observed detail, status (match / mismatch / unverified), and action. This is a factual comparison, not a fidelity percentage. Use [detail review template](product-detail-review.md) when many small controls or labels matter.

Check the actual asset's format and dimensions rather than trusting its URL extension. A manufacturer's regional web page does not prove the pictured keyboard's regional SKU. Preserve the view supported by the reference; a front view does not establish the back or internal components. If small print cannot be read reliably, mark it unverified even when the overall image looks convincing. A mismatch in a required detail blocks finished-ad approval; unresolved fine detail limits the result to a concept pending product-owner review.
