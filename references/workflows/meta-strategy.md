# Strategy and measurement
Operational extension with creative alignment from T01-T03/T07-T08. Start from the outcome and economics. Verify an available objective/event/destination for sales, qualified leads, app outcomes or messaging. Do not substitute cheap engagement for the actual goal. Map awareness to message, landing page and follow-up. Check inventory, capacity, lead handling and exclusions.
Assess Pixel/CAPI/events as applicable, deduplication, consent, landing events, UTMs, CRM/order reconciliation, attribution, currency and timezone. A checklist is not proof tracking works: obtain authorized evidence before declaring readiness.
Propose the simplest supported structure, audience constraints, placements, conversion event, budget mode, schedule and special-category requirements. Messaging pools do not require ad-set fragmentation. Verify current targeting/Advantage+ controls. Set a proposed test cap, primary outcome, guardrails, variable, evidence requirement, observation window and review point. Distinguish proposals from authorized spend. Deliver strategy, media plan, measurement gaps and test matrix.
