# Business research
Operational extension applying T01/T02/T03. Inspect the supplied website, product, reviews, support materials, prior ads and authorized history. Browse current public facts and competitors, recording URLs and dates. Competitor ads show messaging, not profitability. Do not invent customer quotes or copy competitor assets.
Build product/offer facts, geography/language, price/AOV, contribution economics, capacity/inventory, sales cycle, business objective, destination and measurement constraints. Separate facts from unknowns. For each audience pool record jobs, pains, desires, awareness, triggers, objections and evidence. Apply T02 problem expansion, feature-to-angle, objection flips, emotional ladder and competitor gaps. Rank opportunities with confidence and reasoning. Deliver business brief, audience map, evidence table, claim register and creative hypotheses. Never assume the plugin owner's business is the client.

## Ideal customer and motivation deliverables
Use customer-offer-intelligence.md for ranked ideal segments, buyer roles, explicit qualification/disqualification, practical/emotional/social motivations, purchase triggers, objections and evidence confidence. Deliver a useful recommended starting segment and offer hypothesis, not just a generic audience list. Use Web Search when current evidence is needed, following ../knowledge/native-capabilities.md.



## Proportionate research
For a fictional or narrowly scoped copy brief, start from supplied facts and explicitly labelled customer hypotheses. Browse only when a current factual claim or genuinely missing decision requires it. Do not turn every short copy request into an exhaustive market-research task. With real products preserve source date, exact variant and locale; resolve conflicting volatile claims before using them.
