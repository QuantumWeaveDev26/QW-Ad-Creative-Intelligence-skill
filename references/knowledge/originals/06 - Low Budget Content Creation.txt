# Low Budget Content Creation

## Summary
### Flow Summary
High quality content and ads can be shot on a low budget: prioritize an iPhone, a microphone (1000 से 5000), a tripod, natural light, a minimal background and Instagram Edits.

### Core Equipment
- A studio or three point lighting is not required; a decorated corner at home can serve as a studio, since creativity is the key.
- Camera and microphone are the two must-have investments because they decide whether content looks premium.
- Invest at least in an iPhone; a second hand one works fine. This video itself was shot on an iPhone 13.
- Audio quality matters a lot; starter microphones are available within 1000 से 5000.
    - Example: a Godox microphone under 3000, good for phone recording
- A tripod is a must because there is not always a camera person; Amazon Basics ones cost around 700-600.
- All equipment mentioned will be listed in the video description.

### Audio Cleanup and Editing Apps
- Noisy or distorted audio can be cleaned with AI tools like Adobe Podcast: log in, upload the audio file, then overlay it on the video.
- Mobile editors suggested: VN Video Editor, InShot, and the favourite, Instagram's Edits app, used in their agency.
- Edits is free and offers captions generation, editing, filters, audio enhance and audio cleaning; a PC version is expected soon.

### Lighting
- Sunlight is the best light source; face the light so it falls directly on your face, never stand with the sun behind you.
- Standing beside a window with the curtain open gives soft indirect sunlight on the face.
- Golden hour (morning and evening) gives a soothing, beautiful effect; avoid midday because harsh light overexposes footage.
- For night shoots, a small ring light costing ₹800 or less (700 से ₹800) gives enough glow.

### Background and Outdoor Shoots
- Keep backgrounds minimalist so they do not pull audience focus away from you; the background defines your personality.
- Outdoor shooting is the best low budget option: parks, under trees, leaning on bikes, walking shots, with creativity as the only limit.
- Main outdoor drawback is an uncontrolled environment with traffic and passersby noise.
    - Mitigate by using empty parking spaces, parks or spots away from main roads

### Practice and Taste
- Regular practice teaches you your environment and best practices over time.
- Consume similar content on Instagram and YouTube, following low budget and outdoor creators, to develop taste for backgrounds.
