Track the presence and position of tiny printing separately from its wording. Removing a real print block is a mismatch even if its words are unreadable. Obtain a better approved source or an explicitly authorized workflow that preserves the original product pixels. Until then keep exact product reproduction blocked, while delivering useful copy or a clearly labelled concept.
