# Review the complete funnel

Use for a complete funnel or when an ad, offer or CTA changes. This is an operational extension of the training's one-message/one-action principles, not a transcript-prescribed landing-page formula.

## Follow the buyer's next step
Compare the actual ad, destination, form, acknowledgement and follow-up together. For each transition check the intended buyer, promised deliverable, primary action, essential qualification, price/terms and conditions. Read the rendered image too when supplied. A shared campaign name does not establish continuity.

If a variant changes the requested action, adapt its destination. A swatch-enquiry ad needs an immediate way to ask about the swatch and its terms; the unchanged quote-focused hero may not satisfy that promise. If the destination variant is not written or implemented, mark that variant pending rather than claiming a complete funnel. An angle change can share a page when the promised next step and evidence stay the same.

Use the actual workflow to distinguish enquiry, qualified enquiry, confirmed appointment/demo, suitability decision, accepted job/order, completed work and collected cash. An assessment enquiry does not establish a booking, successful repair or a free visit. Name only stages relevant to this business; do not force a purchase flow onto an assessment service.

## Preserve the mechanism and material conditions
- Carry useful fit conditions through the funnel: service area, dimensions/quantity, owner transport, suitability, variant or mandatory capability.
- A CSV import feature is not evidence of every file schema working or a continuous external-system integration. State the feature actually supplied and ask about file structure when it changes fit; avoid requesting private customer exports just to qualify a lead.
- Do not add a price, included service, trial, visit charge, pickup, SLA, warranty, outcome or certification in a later stage to make the funnel look complete. Resolve the fact or omit the claim and give usable supported copy.
- Keep hypotheses about buyer motives separate from customer research actually performed. The next validation step can be a customer conversation or review of existing enquiries; it need not be an ad-spend recommendation.

## Make forms and messages work together
Ask for the minimum information that changes the next decision. In an acknowledgement, use answers already received and ask only the next unanswered useful question. Provide conditional message variants when necessary; a fixed draft that repeats every form field is not ready for automatic use.

Separate proposed follow-up handling from an established business promise. State trigger, contact/consent context, timing, exact wording, next action and stop condition. A reminder schedule is not a response or completion SLA. A sent enquiry, working form, booked slot or delivered message must not be inferred from copy. Business-approved handling and actual execution remain separate checks.

## Measure the promised stage
Define qualification and each denominator before comparing variants. Deduplicate enquiries by a documented identity/window; compare like cohorts and do not count the same person as a new qualified lead after every reply. Track assessment enquiries separately from accepted jobs and collections. Booked value is not collected cash; cash after ads alone is not profit. Without the relevant downstream data, identify what is unknown rather than declaring a winner from CTR.

## Deliver the useful result
Return the recommended complete funnel, then concise material gaps and a next test. Say which alternate variants are complete, pending a destination change, or rejected for unsupported claims. Report manual semantic review separately from automated package/retrieval checks and from live performance. Do not grade an entire funnel as correct merely because all sections exist.
