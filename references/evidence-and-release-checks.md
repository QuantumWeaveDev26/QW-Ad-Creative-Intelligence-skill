# Evidence, product identity and delivery status
Apply proportionally when handling real products, commercial claims or measured results. Small fictional copy tasks do not require a full research report. These controls extend the transcript principles; they are not assertions that every operational detail appears in the course.

## Anchor facts before writing
For material product/offer claims record the exact product/SKU or variant, source URL or client document, retrieved date, locale, current value and any condition. Separate manufacturer claims, customer observations, user-supplied facts, inferred motivations and measured test results. A feature on a competitor page is not evidence of the client's feature, demand size or a market gap.

If fetched pages disagree on price, stock, discount, shipping or terms, do not pick the more attractive value. Omit that claim from the finished creative or obtain current confirmation for the intended market and variant. Preserve qualifications: glass being dishwasher-safe does not make its rubber gasket dishwasher-safe. A requested slogan is not evidence that the claim is true. Deliver a useful corrected alternative rather than only refusing or filling the copy with placeholders.

## Use the correct real product image
Record source/provenance and inspect the image before generation. Verify exact edition, colour, packaging, artwork, visible controls, dimensions/proportions and supplied viewpoint. A similar product from the same series is not an acceptable substitute. Keep identity-critical details prominent enough to review. Do not invent unseen surfaces or features; choose a viewpoint supported by references. A low-resolution source limits fine-detail claims.

Use a real reference as the image tool input, not just a product name in the prompt. Separate edit targets, product references and mood references. Ask for product-preserving edits and check the result against the original. Native image generation may redraw details: never claim pixel-identical preservation from a visual match. If exact packaging/artwork reproduction is required and a generative edit drifts, mark the result incomplete and use an explicitly available authorized compositing workflow or request the necessary asset; do not quietly publish the approximation. Never use a test concept as evidence of actual product quality or results.

Manufacturer images or brand marks used for a private evaluation do not establish commercial reuse rights or brand endorsement. Keep independent tests labelled and private. For a client's authorized production materials, apply the client's actual brand requirements rather than inserting irrelevant test disclaimers.

## Keep copy useful when facts are incomplete
Produce the requested headline, primary text, CTA or landing copy at the requested scope. Omit an unsupported guarantee, process step, turnaround, response time or testimonial instead of inventing it to fill a template. Ask for only the missing fact that blocks use. Offer a fact-bounded version and a short list of remaining decisions. CTA wording is creative copy unless a supported Meta button/schema has actually been checked. Do not promise working forms or automations from copy drafts.

## Keep data meanings intact
Record whether clicks are all clicks, link clicks or outbound clicks. State the definition used; do not relabel generic input. Define lead and qualified lead, time window, currency and attribution. Missing metadata may allow arithmetic under stated assumptions, but not a confident live-account comparison. Compare weighted totals and business outcomes, not just attention metrics. Equal CPQL with different CTR does not establish a business winner; small, observational samples do not establish causal uplift. Explain the most useful next check without prescribing unauthorized spend.

## Report separate readiness states
- Source integrity: original hashes and timestamps verified where used.
- Skill execution: instructions actually invoked/applied and outputs inspected.
- Creative QA: text and visible reference identity checked; record limitations.
- Business approval: actual claims, terms, rights and brand approval confirmed or pending.
- Host invocation: actual selected plugin response observed or unverified.
- Integration execution: actual authorized tool read/write observed or untested.
- Live performance: measured or unknown.
Do not roll these into an invented perfect score. A polished image or a successful archive save cannot pass the other states. Preserve the user's decision to defer account access; do not reopen sharing or credentials work as a side effect of quality improvement.
